The Maison

A house of
quiet light.

Hidden along a fictional Mediterranean coastline, Maison Aurelune was imagined as a place where the outside world slows down.

Every space opens naturally toward the sea — from sunlit suites to intimate dining rooms, private gardens and secluded coves.

Nothing here asks for your attention. Everything simply invites you to stay a little longer.
